# 故事大綱 / Story Bible — Grade 1 Visual Novel
### 《念一念》 — *The Reading Book*

> A first-grader's first year at Chinese school. Every chant in the textbook
> becomes a tiny world he steps into. By June, the lonely new kid who sang
> "where are my friends?" can answer: **在這裡 — right here.**

---

## 1. Why this story

The 16 lessons are scattered (numbers, fruit, months, fire, snow, mirrors…),
but the textbook **opens and closes on the same idea**:

- **L1.2** 我的朋友在哪裡 — *Where are my friends?* (a question)
- **L16** 我和我的朋友 — *Me and my friends* — names 明明 / 友友 / 中中 (the answer)

So the whole year is one arc: **from "I have no friends" → "I have many friends."**
Everything in between is the path. That bookend is the spine; the lessons hang on it
in their original order (which also keeps the vocabulary teaching sequence intact).

---

## 2. Core mechanic — the book that comes alive

On day one, shy 明明 finds a glowing storybook in the school library: **《念一念》**.
When he **reads a chant out loud**, the page opens into a little world he can walk
through — the tiger mountain, the fruit tree, the snow field. A squirrel named
**小五** (the 5th squirrel from L1's "松鼠有幾隻？一二三四五") hops out of the book
and becomes his guide.

This gives every lesson a clean reason to exist as a scene:

> **唸出課文 → 進入故事世界 → 玩 → 學會 → 回到學校**
> *Recite the chant → enter its world → play → learn it → return to school.*

The "real world" (school, friends, family) carries the emotional story.
The "book worlds" carry the lessons. 小五 threads the two together.

## 3. Cast (all level-appropriate, drawn from the text)

| Character | 中文 | Role | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ming Ming | 明明 (言立明) | Player / protagonist | Shy, kind, observant → grows brave |
| Youyou | 友友 (林友友) | First friend | Sunny, loud, generous (shares his fruit) |
| Zhongzhong | 中中 (江禾中) | Second friend | Calm, clever, great at games & drawing |
| Teacher | 老師 | Guide / narrator | Warm; "老師的大手" (L3) |
| Little Five | 小五 (小松鼠) | Magic book guide | Cute, mostly mimes & counts; can't be left behind |
| Mom / Dad | 媽媽 / 爸爸 | Home warmth | Appear in the family interlude (L6) |

**Writing rule:** the connective dialogue ("glue") between chants must reuse
**only Grade-1 vocabulary** (words from the chants themselves) plus names. This
keeps the whole game readable by an actual Grade-1 student. English glosses show
under the Chinese, and the player can hide them (Preferences → 英文 English;
see `docs/adr/0002-english-toggle-and-chant-voice.md`).

## 5. The cutscenes (full flow)

Format per beat:
- **▶ CUT-IN** — narrative that leads *into* the chant (the "glue")
- **♪ 念一念** — the chant itself = the interactive learning core
- **◀ CUT-OUT** — transition that hands off to the next beat

Glue dialogue is intentionally tiny and reuses learned words. `[INTERACTIVE]`
marks where the player taps/plays rather than watches.

---

### ▷ OPENING CUTSCENE — 第一天 (The First Day)

`scene: school gate, morning, autumn leaves`

A big building. Children laughing in groups. **明明** stands alone at the gate,
backpack straps gripped tight.

> 明明（心想）：好多人……我不認識他們。
> *(So many people… I don't know any of them.)*

The bell rings. Recess. He drifts away from the noise into the quiet **library**.
A book on a high shelf is glowing softly: **《念一念》**.

He opens it. The room dims; the page brightens. A tiny **squirrel** tumbles out,
lands on the book, looks up at him, and holds up five fingers.

> 小五：一、二、三、四、五！
> 明明：你……會說話？
> *(You… can talk?)*

小五 grabs his sleeve and pulls him *into* the page. → **L1.1**

---

### L1.1 — 上山找老虎  `book world: green mountain`

**▶ CUT-IN** — They land on a cartoon mountain path. 小五 points up the trail and
mimes a tiger's roar, then hides behind 明明's leg.

**♪ 念一念**
```
一二三四五，上山找老虎，
老虎找不到，看見小松鼠。
松鼠有幾隻？一二三四五。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` Climb the path; **tap each squirrel as it pops out** and count
一→五 aloud. No tiger to be found — just five squirrels (小五 is the fifth).

**◀ CUT-OUT** — At the top, 明明 laughs for the first time. 小五 points back down
toward a window of light — the real classroom. But first, a question floats up
from the next page…

---

### L1.2 — 我的朋友在哪裡  `book world fading into → classroom doorway`

**▶ CUT-IN** — The page turns itself. The word **朋友** glows. 明明's smile fades —
he remembers he's the new kid.

**♪ 念一念**
```
一二三四五六七，
我的朋友在哪裡，
在這裡，在這裡，
我的朋友在這裡。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` Sing-along, line by line (karaoke highlight 一→七). On the last
line the game asks: **「你的朋友在哪裡？」** — and the screen shows… no one yet.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Back in the real library, the bell rings. A boy's voice calls from
the hall: *"喂——!"* A bright-faced kid (**友友**) waves at him — then his class
lines up and he's swept away. 明明 reaches out a beat too late.

> 明明（心想）：我的朋友……在哪裡？
> *(The question is now the player's, too.)*

---

### L2.1 — 一棵樹上十樣果  `real: classroom, snack time → book world: fruit tree`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Snack time. 明明 has no snack. He opens 《念一念》 under the desk;
小五 points at a page bursting with fruit. They step in — a tree hung with ten kinds.

**♪ 念一念**
```
一二三四五 六七八九十，
一棵樹上十樣果，你說稀奇不稀奇？
蘋果、香蕉、葡萄、橘子、番茄、桃子、
杏子、木瓜、李子、梨。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Shake the tree**; ten fruits fall — tap & name each, counting 六→十.
*(Built: tap the tree three times, then the `tap_count` game with ten fruits; each
fruit says its name when tapped.)*

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 小五 stuffs its cheeks. 明明 catches one last 蘋果… and back in
class, it's real, in his hand. A shadow falls over his desk —

---

### L2.2 — 握握手  `real: classroom`

**▶ CUT-IN** — It's **友友** from the hallway. He grins and holds out his own snack.

> 友友：給你！ 明明：謝謝……
> 友友：我叫友友。你呢？ 明明：我……我叫明明。

**♪ 念一念**
```
一二三，握握手。 四五六，點點頭。
七八九，大步走。 十百千，走圓圈。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` Follow the actions on beat — **握手 / 點頭 / 走** — tapping in time.
On "握握手," 明明 and 友友 shake hands. **(First friend — half won.)**
*(Built as the `pick_one` game: pick the words that finish each line; the right
answer replays that chant line. They swap snacks first — 香蕉 for 蘋果.)*

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 友友 spots the squirrel peeking from the book. *"哇! 你的書會動!"*
He's delighted, not scared. A calmer boy at the next desk looks over, curious — **中中**.

---

### L3.1 — 拍拍手  `real: classroom, 老師 enters`

**▶ CUT-IN** — **老師** claps to start class. *"小朋友，看老師的手——"*

**♪ 念一念**
```
老師的大手拍一拍，小朋友的小手拍一拍。
上拍三下，下拍三下，
中間用力拍一下，大家一起坐下來。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Clap-pattern game** (上三下三中一). 友友 plays loud, 中中 plays
perfectly — 明明 copies 中中 and they share a look. **(Three threads beginning to braid.)**

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 老師: *"今天我們學——一年有幾個月。"* She unrolls a big calendar.

---

### L3.2 — 一年有幾個月  `real: classroom, calendar`

**▶ CUT-IN** — 小五 hops along the calendar, tapping each month.

**♪ 念一念**
```
一年有十二個月。
一月大，二月小，三月大，四月小，
五月大，六月小，七月大，八月大，
九月小，十月大，十一月小，十二月大。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Sort the 12 months in order**; mark each 大/小. The filled calendar
becomes the game's progress map — *"我們的一年" (our year)* — foreshadowing the journey.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 老師 points to next period: **畫畫課** (art).

---

### L4.1 — 我有十個手指頭  `real: art room`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Hands out, paint ready. 老師: *"先數一數你的手指頭。"*

**♪ 念一念**
```
我有十個手指頭，五個在左、五個在右。
會寫字、會拍球，就是不開口。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` Count fingers **五個在左 / 五個在右**; sort actions onto the right hand.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 友友 flicks paint at 明明 and laughs. *"我們來畫畫!"*

---

### L4.2 — 畫張畫・小豬  `real: art room`

**▶ CUT-IN** — 友友 and 明明 share one big sheet; 中中 leans in to watch.

**♪ 念一念**
```
胖胖身體像冬瓜，尖尖耳朵「工」嘴巴，
小小眼睛四條腿，最後別忘加尾巴。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Draw the pig step by step** (身體→耳朵→嘴巴→眼睛→腿→尾巴), each
stroke triggered by its line. Two pigs, side by side, both a bit lopsided.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — They hold up their drawings and crack up. The window darkens — clouds.
小五 tugs the book open to a steep, dramatic page… **adventure.**

> *End of Act 1 — 明明 has two almost-friends and a magic squirrel.*

---

### L5.1 — 小人兒上大山  `book world: tall mountain, dramatic`

**▶ CUT-IN** — The biggest world yet. 小五 points up — a red glow at the summit.

**♪ 念一念**
```
啊！火！ 大火！ 山上大火！ 下山！下山！
小人兒下大山，
啊！水！ 大水！ 山下大水！ 上山！上山！
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Run the right way** — when 火 appears at the top, swipe **下山**;
when 水 floods the bottom, swipe **上山**. A genuine little set-piece (the one place
a short AI-generated **video clip** would shine).

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Safe on a ledge, panting. Night falls. The danger drains away into
quiet. 小五 curls up. → calm page.

---

### L5.2 — 晚上  `book world: night, moon, stars`

**▶ CUT-IN** — A still, silver scene. A little wooden house in the forest.

**♪ 念一念**
```
山頂尖尖，土路彎彎，森林裡有間方方的小木屋。
天上有個圓圓的大月亮，還有許多小星星。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Tap the stars** to connect them; the moon rises. Calm, breathing-room
beat. The book gently closes itself.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 明明 looks up — he's home, at his own window, same moon. 媽媽 calls him.

---

### L6.1 — 媽媽的生日  `real: home`

**▶ CUT-IN** — A calendar at home: tomorrow is **媽媽的生日**. 明明 wants to make a card.
He texts his new friends; 友友 and 中中 come over to help. **(Friendship leaves school.)**

**♪ 念一念**
```
今天是媽媽的生日，我送媽媽一張卡片，
爸爸送媽媽一朵小紅花，媽媽很快樂。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Make the card** — choose 卡片, add 一朵小紅花; give it to 媽媽.
媽媽 很快樂. (First time the trio does something together outside class.)

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Friends head home; *"明天學校見!"* 明明 sets his alarm.

---

### L6.2 — 起得早  `real: home → street, dawn`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Sunrise. The 太陽 is up before him.

**♪ 念一念**
```
太陽起得早，我也起得早，天天早起上學校。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Beat the sun** — tap to get up, dressed, out the door before it
fully rises. He runs to school, where 友友 & 中中 wait at the gate.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — They walk a garden path to school. Flowers everywhere.

---

### L7.1 — 紅花和白花  `real: garden path`

**▶ CUT-IN** — 中中 notices the flowers differ on the hill above vs. below.

**♪ 念一念**
```
山上有紅花，也有白花。 山下有紅花，也有白花。
山上的紅花少，白花多。 山下的紅花多，白花少。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Count & compare** 紅花/白花, 山上/山下; pick which side has 多 / 少.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 烏雲 rolls in. First drops fall.

---

### L7.2 — 下雨了  `real: garden, rain begins`

**▶ CUT-IN** — *"下雨了!"* They run for cover under a tree.

**♪ 念一念**
```
烏雲來了，下雨了！下雨了！
雨下在花上，雨下在樹上，雨也下在草原上。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Tap where the rain falls** — 花上 / 樹上 / 草原上 — watch each
bloom react. The rain gets heavier…

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Real downpour. They sprint inside. Recess trapped indoors.

---

### L8.1 — 大風和大雨  `real: indoors, storm outside`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Faces at the window. 友友 groans: *"我想去公園踢足球!"*

**♪ 念一念**
```
大風，大雨，快走開，請你明天再來。
我想去公園踢足球，我想去海邊玩沙子。
大風，大雨，快走開，請你明天再來。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` Chant-shout at the storm; **match each wish** (公園/足球, 海邊/沙子).
The rain won't quit — so 中中 grins: *"我們來猜拳!"*

**◀ CUT-OUT** — They sit in a circle on the floor.

---

### L8.2 — 來猜拳  `real: indoors`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Hands behind backs. *"好朋友，我們行個禮——"*

**♪ 念一念**
```
好朋友我們行個禮，握握手呀來猜拳，
剪刀石頭布看誰贏，輸了就要跟我走。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Play 剪刀石頭布** vs 友友 & 中中, best of three. Whoever loses
becomes the line-leader. Laughing in a circle. **(The trio is now truly a trio.)**
Callback: the word **好朋友** glows — the L1.2 question is being answered.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Lunch bell. They line up for rice.

---

### L9 — 一粒米  `real: lunchroom`

**▶ CUT-IN** — 明明, full and distracted, pushes away a bowl with rice left in it.
小五 peeks out, looks sad, and points to a single grain. The book glows; the grain
*speaks* (a small daydream).

**♪ 念一念**
```
我是一粒小白米，沒有什麼了不起。可是——
農人種田很辛苦，米飯得來不容易。
小朋友，請珍惜！好好吃飯，不要剩飯粒。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` A gentle scene: see the 農人 work; then **clear every 飯粒** from
the bowl. A quiet values beat — no scolding, just understanding.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 明明 finishes his rice. Snow starts to fall outside the window —
the season turns. → **Act 3, Winter.**

---

### L10 — 猴子穿新衣  `real: week montage`

**▶ CUT-IN** — A playful narrator-monkey from the book hosts a **whole-week montage**
of the trio's new winter routine.

**♪ 念一念**
```
星期一，猴子在家穿新衣。 星期二，猴子叫著肚子餓。
星期三，猴子一起去爬山。 星期四，猴子坐著看電視。
星期五，猴子唱歌又跳舞。 星期六，猴子生火來烤肉。
星期日，猴子拍手過生日。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Drag each activity onto the right day** 星期一→日. Each correct
day plays a 2-second clip of the monkey (and the three kids mirroring it).

**◀ CUT-OUT** — On 星期日's party, a small sour note: someone (an offscreen classmate)
says something unkind. 友友 frowns. → senses & words.

---

### L11.1 — 五官和雙手  `real: classroom`

**▶ CUT-IN** — 老師 steadies the mood: *"我們有五官，也有雙手——它們會做什麼？"*

**♪ 念一念**
```
小小眼睛，看東看西。 小小舌頭，舔來舔去。
小小鼻子，聞聞香氣。 小小耳朵，聽聽聲音。
小小牙齒，愛咬東西。 小小雙手，寫寫畫畫。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Match each sense to what it does** (眼→看, 耳→聽, 鼻→聞…).

**◀ CUT-OUT** — 老師: *"那……我們的口，要說什麼話？"*

---

### L11.2 — 口說好話  `real: classroom`

**▶ CUT-IN** — A gentle lesson aimed at the earlier unkind words.

**♪ 念一念**
```
我的口說好話，我的手做好事，
我的心想好念，我的腳走正路。
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Choose 好話 / 好事** in little situations; 明明 says a kind word to
the upset classmate. The cloud lifts. **(Ming Ming, once too shy to speak, now speaks first.)**

**◀ CUT-OUT** — School lets out into deep snow. 友友: *"下雪了!! 我們出去!"*

---

### L12.1 — 小狗和小雞  `real: snow field`

**▶ CUT-IN** — Fresh white field. 小五 leaves tiny prints. The kids notice prints
look like drawings.

**♪ 念一念**
```
小狗和小雞在雪地上走。
小雞說:「你看，你看，我會畫竹葉。」
小狗說:「你看，你看，我會畫梅花。」
```
`[INTERACTIVE]` **Stamp prints in the snow** — chicken-feet make 竹葉, dog-paws make
梅花; "paint" a picture with footprints.

**◀ CUT-OUT** — Then they build something bigger together…

---

### L12.2 — 雪人怎麼不見了  `real: snow field → next day`

**▶ CUT-IN** — They build a **雪人** (give it a face, twig arms — *"沒有腳呀"*). They
name it. Time-skip: sun comes out.

**♪ 念一念**
```
雪人怎麼不見了？ 雪人怎麼不見了？
雪人只有手呀沒有腳呀，他呀他呀不會自己跑。
雪人只怕太陽照，太陽一出雪就消，
```

## 6. Production notes (how this maps to the game)

- **Two visual registers.** *Real world* (school/home) = warm, grounded children's-book
  illustration. *Book worlds* = brighter, storybook-fantasy with a paper/page texture.
  小五 and the glowing book are the visual bridge between them.
- **Where AI video earns its keep (≈5 clips, not 24):** the Opening, **L5.1 fire/flood**,
  the **L10 week montage**, **L12.2 snowman melting**, and the **L15 friends-light-up**
  + Ending. Everything else = stills + simple animation (AnimateDiff / Live2D-style sway).
- **Per-lesson loop is identical**, so it's cheap to template in Ren'Py:
  `CUT-IN (story) → 念一念 (read + interactive game) → reward star → CUT-OUT (transition)`.
- **Progress map** = the L3.2 twelve-month calendar; finishing a lesson lights a month.
- **The bookend is the emotional engine** — keep L1.2 and L16 tightly mirrored
  ("在哪裡?" → "在這裡"). Don't lose that thread when scripting.
- **Glue stays in Grade-1 vocabulary.** If a connective line needs a word not yet taught,
  rewrite it or move it into a later lesson's glue.

## 7. Open choices for you

1. **Art style** — soft children's-book vs. brighter anime-chibi.
2. **Voice** — full TTS narration of every chant (recommended for a *reading* game) vs. text-only.
3. **Squirrel 小五** — keep the magic-book framing, or play it straight (no fantasy, lessons
   as plain school activities)? The framing is what makes the scattered chants feel like one story.
4. **Scope of v1** — ship **Act 1 (Lessons 1–4)** as a polished vertical slice first, then expand.
